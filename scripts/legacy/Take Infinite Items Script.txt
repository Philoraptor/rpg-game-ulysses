Take Infinite Amount of Items (TakeObj) *Credits to Matt Dauler (Chuck)
====================================

Function Main(Player as Long) As Long
Dim A as Long, B as Long, Item as Long

Item = 6 'Change this to your item number you want to take away

For A = 1 To HasObj(Player, Item)
	B = B + 1
Next A

PlayerMessage(Player, "Found " + Str(B) + " items.", White)
TakeObj(Player, Item, B)

End Function
